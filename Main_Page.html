📖 HOW TO PLAY & GAME DETAILS

🔤 BASIC RULES

Unscramble the letters to form the correct word before the timer runs out. Each correct word earns points. Use hints carefully – they cost a key or reduce health depending on the mode.

🕹️ CLASSIC MODE – STRUCTURED PROGRESSION

Classic Mode is based on a clear six - level structure: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2. Each level has a countdown timer. Three keys (hints) are provided per level – using a hint costs one key. If the timer runs out, you must restart the level. This mode is like a skill ladder: simple, short words at lower levels, longer and more complex words at higher levels. Clear milestones, visible progress, and growing challenge keep you motivated to unlock higher levels.

⚡ ENDLESS MODE – SURVIVAL CHALLENGE

Endless Mode has no level limits – only your high score matters. It uses a blood percentage system (health). You start with full blood. Using a hint or giving a wrong answer decreases your blood percentage. Solve words to earn points and survive as long as possible. When blood reaches 0%, the game ends. After a cooldown of about 45 minutes, your blood refills and you can continue from your previous score, aiming to beat your best.

🏆 DAILY CHALLENGE – HABIT BUILDER

One puzzle per day. Hints are not allowed. Winning increases your streak, losing resets it to zero. Your trophy count shows total successful streaks. Designed to build daily learning habits, the streak system encourages you to return each day. The risk of losing your streak adds excitement and keeps you engaged.
